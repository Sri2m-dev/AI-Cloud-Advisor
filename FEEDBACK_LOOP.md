# User/Client Feedback Loop Template

## After Each Phase
1. Prepare a short survey (Google Form, Typeform, or in-app):
   - What worked well in this release?
   - Any blockers or confusing areas?
   - What’s the most valuable feature so far?
   - What’s missing or needs improvement?
2. Schedule a 15–30 min feedback/demo session with key users.
3. Summarize findings in a doc and adjust the next phase plan accordingly.

Copy and adapt this for each phase.
