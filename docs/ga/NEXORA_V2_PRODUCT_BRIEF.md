# Nexora v2.0 Product Brief

## The executive problem

Technology decisions are fragmented across finance, architecture, operations, cloud,
SaaS, risk, and workflow systems. Executives receive dashboards and recommendations but
often cannot see the evidence, uncertainty, decision authority, or verified outcome in one
place.

## The Nexora answer

Nexora is an evidence-backed technology decision control plane. It composes certified
enterprise evidence into a governed story: what changed, why it matters, what is at risk,
what action is recommended, who can decide, and how the outcome will be verified.

## Three decisions

1. Approve, defer, resize, or reject a technology investment.
2. Retain, consolidate, modernize, renegotiate, or retire portfolio assets.
3. Understand and mitigate material business-service technology risk.

## Why customers care

- Faster cross-functional decisions with less manual reconciliation.
- Traceable financial, operational, architectural, and risk evidence.
- Explicit `UNKNOWN`, partial, stale, and conflicted states instead of fabricated certainty.
- Human approval and audit continuity from recommendation through verified outcome.
- A consistent executive experience across CIO, CFO, CEO, architecture, operations, and
  FinOps stakeholders.

## Trust model

Nexora does not replace customer systems of record and AI does not make executive
decisions. Tenant isolation, RBAC, certified sources, evidence provenance, confidence,
freshness, and decision authority remain visible. Synthetic demonstration data is opt-in,
immutable, labeled, and restricted to `demo-*` tenants.

## Evaluation

An enterprise can evaluate Nexora through a bounded decision pilot using its own evidence
and the three journeys above. Commercial pricing and ROI are validated against customer
scope, integration effort, finance-approved baselines, and verified benefits; they are not
represented as universal product claims.
