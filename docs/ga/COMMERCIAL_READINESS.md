# Nexora v2.0 Commercial Readiness

## CIO pitch

Nexora turns fragmented technology, finance, architecture, operations, and risk evidence into
an accountable executive decision context. It sits above existing systems of record, explains
what changed and why it matters, preserves uncertainty, and connects recommendations to human
approval and verified outcomes.

## Demonstration script (45 minutes maximum)

1. **Trust model — 5 minutes:** certified sources, `UNKNOWN`, evidence, confidence, tenancy,
   human authority, and the synthetic-tenant label.
2. **Technology investment — 10 minutes:** NXR-INV-204, checkout dependency, financial impact,
   alternatives, evidence, controls, and approval.
3. **Portfolio rationalization — 10 minutes:** NXR-PORT-118, redundant SaaS, usage, spend,
   ownership, consolidation, and benefit state.
4. **Executive risk — 10 minutes:** NXR-RISK-071, payment-vendor concentration, affected
   service, mitigation, uncertainty, and owner.
5. **Board evidence — 10 minutes:** governed narrative, report/export, chronology, and pilot.

## ROI calculator

This is a customer business-case method, not a product claim.

```text
Annual verified benefit = finance-validated savings + validated avoided cost
                          + approved productivity benefit
Annual program cost = subscription + implementation + customer operating effort
ROI % = (annual verified benefit - annual program cost) / annual program cost * 100
Payback months = annual program cost / annual verified benefit * 12
```

Potential savings must never be entered as realized benefit. Assumption, owner, source,
baseline date, measurement period, and finance approval are mandatory.

## FAQ

**Is Nexora another system of record?** No; it composes certified evidence.

**Does AI decide?** No; humans approve and govern execution.

**What happens when data is missing?** Nexora reports `UNKNOWN` or the appropriate state.

**How is demo data protected?** It is immutable, opt-in, and restricted to `demo-*` tenants.

**How is value measured?** From customer baselines and finance-validated realized outcomes.
**What is excluded?** New intelligence frameworks, autonomous remediation, and v3.0 modules.
