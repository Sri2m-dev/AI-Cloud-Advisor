## Increment principle

Each increment delivers usable, governed value and leaves the platform releasable. Increments are outcome gates, not fixed calendar quarters.

## Increment 0 — Governed baseline

**Value:** a safe starting point for every future team.
**Work packages:** WP-001 to WP-003.
**Outputs:** released v1.2.0 baseline, golden compatibility fixtures, tenant/authorization standards, contract/event governance.
**Exit:** G1/G2/G3 complete; baseline reproducible; no unresolved authority for the next slice.

## Increment 1 — Trusted Business Service context

**Value:** service owners and architects can see which technology supports a critical service and assess evidence quality.
**Work packages:** WP-004 to WP-007.
**Scope:** selected lighthouse sources/services, canonical coverage, ownership and dimensional posture.
**Exit:** accepted coverage/freshness, reconciled ownership, tenant tests, evidence drill-through and steward workflow.

## Increment 2 — Explainable enterprise knowledge

**Value:** authorized dependency, impact and evidence questions are reproducible.
**Work packages:** WP-008 to WP-010.
**Scope:** named queries for lighthouse services; projection rebuild/reconciliation; evidence roles.
**Exit:** no canonical bypass, bounded query performance, temporal/freshness disclosure, graph replay and authorization tests.

## Increment 3 — Governed decision lifecycle

**Value:** one optimization or modernization case moves from alternatives through accountable decision and verified outcome.
**Work packages:** WP-011 to WP-013.
**Exit:** separation of proposer/approver/executor/verifier, policy indeterminate behavior, approval expiry, rollback/compensation and outcome verification.

## Increment 4 — Cross-domain decision products

**Value:** FinOps, portfolio and risk owners use the shared Decision model for real decisions.
**Work packages:** WP-014 and WP-015 plus targeted WP-019 experiences.
**Exit:** finance reconciliation, domain profile acceptance, realized-value/risk measures and legacy parity for selected journeys.

## Increment 5 — Enterprise Memory and evaluated AI

**Value:** future analysis uses reviewed organizational outcomes with transparent applicability.
**Work packages:** WP-016 and WP-017.
**Exit:** Memory admission/expiry/contradiction, authorized retrieval, task evaluations, abstention, prompt-injection/tenant tests and cost budgets.

## Increment 6 — Bounded orchestration and platform certification

**Value:** approved low-risk actions can execute safely and the platform demonstrates enterprise operations.
**Work packages:** WP-018 to WP-020.
**Exit:** explicit autonomy class, dry-run, kill switch, compensation, scale/recovery evidence, runbooks and support ownership.

## Increment governance

At each gate review value evidence, architecture/ADR conformance, security/tenant results, compatibility/migration, SLO/cost, operational readiness, debt and rollback. Failure returns the increment for remediation; it does not silently defer a mandatory control.

## Usable-value rule

No increment closes solely on internal platform components. It must demonstrate a named persona decision or operating outcome, even when the visible experience is deliberately narrow.
