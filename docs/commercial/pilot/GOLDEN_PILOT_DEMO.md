# Nexora Golden Pilot Demo

## Objective

Demonstrate one coherent enterprise decision story using governed customer evidence.

The Golden Pilot Demo is not a tour of every Nexora page.

Core story:

**Evidence -> Intelligence -> Ask -> Decision -> Governance**

## Step 1 - Customer Context

Establish:

- customer organization;
- persona;
- pilot objective;
- in-scope environment;
- onboarding mode;
- evidence period.

## Step 2 - Analyze Environment

Use the approved onboarding mode.

For Zero-Access, process the approved evidence package.

For AWS Connected Mode, use the customer-created read-only IAM role after environment prerequisites are complete.

Show:

- governed admission;
- evidence coverage;
- SourceFacts;
- important quality findings;
- provenance.

## Step 3 - Executive Experience

Show the customer's technology and financial posture using only evidence-supported information.

Avoid representing partial evidence as complete enterprise visibility.

## Step 4 - Ask Nexora

Ask customer-relevant questions covering:

- inventory/environment;
- cost;
- application or business relationships;
- ownership;
- optimization;
- one deliberately unsupported question.

Use the unsupported question to demonstrate UNKNOWN behavior.

Nexora must not invent missing evidence.

## Step 5 - Decision Intelligence

Show how an evidence-backed finding can progress into recommendation or decision context.

Show:

- supporting evidence;
- provenance;
- confidence/coverage where available;
- accountable next action;
- human authority.

## Step 6 - Reporting / Outcome

Review customer-consumable output where the environment supports it.

Separate:

- observed facts;
- findings;
- evidence gaps;
- qualifications;
- next actions.

## Close

Summarize:

- what Nexora knows;
- why Nexora knows it;
- what remains UNKNOWN;
- which decisions the evidence supports;
- what additional evidence would improve intelligence;
- whether the customer should remain in evidence-import mode or progress to Connected Mode.

## Commercial Progression

**Upload -> Prove -> Connect -> Automate**

## Guardrails

Do not:

- expose credentials;
- bypass tenant boundaries;
- hide UNKNOWN;
- claim unsupported live integrations;
- claim savings before verified realization;
- claim production load/SLA certification unless separately validated;
- represent synthetic figures as customer outcomes;
- imply automatic remediation without authorization.
