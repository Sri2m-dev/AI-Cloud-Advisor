# Nexora v2.0 Buyer FAQ

## What is Nexora?

Nexora is an Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform. It connects enterprise technology,
finance, architecture, operations, and risk evidence so leaders can make defensible technology
decisions from one governed context.

## Is Nexora another dashboard or enterprise architecture repository?

No. Dashboards and registries describe the estate. Nexora composes those certified facts into
decision stories: what changed, why it matters, what action is recommended, what happens if the
organization delays, and what financial evidence supports the choice.

## Which decisions does v2.0 support?

The launch demonstration centers on three repeatable decisions:

- technology investment and modernization;
- application, technology, and SaaS portfolio rationalization;
- executive technology-risk mitigation and acceptance.

## Does Nexora replace ServiceNow, LeanIX, Apptio, or cloud-management tools?

Nexora is designed to compose governed evidence from systems of record rather than recreate
every source capability. Its differentiation is the cross-domain decision layer joining
technology, finance, risk, architecture, operations, recommendations, and accountable action.

## How does Nexora prevent misleading executive metrics?

Executive values must come from certified upstream sources. Missing data is shown as `UNKNOWN`,
not zero or an estimate. Opportunity and realized value are separate states. Narratives, KPIs,
visuals, and reports use the same evidence rules.

## Can synthetic demonstration data leak into production?

The demo tenant requires explicit demo mode, a `demo-*` tenant identifier, and mandatory
synthetic classification. Production tenants cannot load the bundled demonstration dataset.

## How are recommendations explained?

Recommendations retain evidence coverage, confidence, source context, business-service impact,
material unknowns, and the human authority required for approval. AI assistance does not remove
accountability.

## How does Nexora protect tenants and roles?

Tenant context and role entitlements are enforced before executive composition. Navigation and
canonical intelligence surfaces are filtered to the authenticated persona. Product Completion
does not alter those frozen security boundaries.

## What would a pilot prove?

A bounded pilot should prove one or more named decisions using customer-approved sources:
decision-cycle time, evidence completeness, stakeholder alignment, identified opportunity,
verified value, or reduced risk exposure. Commercial outcomes must be baselined with the customer.

## Does Nexora guarantee savings?

No. The platform distinguishes identified, evidence-qualified, approved, executed, and verified
realized value. Any business case depends on customer inputs, adoption, execution, and agreed
measurement methods.

## What is required to deploy Nexora?

Deployment requirements depend on the approved customer topology and source integrations. The
v2.0 release package includes installation, configuration, operational, backup, restore, upgrade,
and rollback guidance. Production credentials and customer data are never part of demo assets.

## What happens after v2.0?

Post-GA development is driven by customer evidence. Product Completion does not introduce v3.0
frameworks or speculative platform expansion.
