# Nexora v2.0 Executive Demonstration

## Purpose

This 35-minute demonstration shows how Nexora turns fragmented technology, finance, risk,
and architecture evidence into three accountable executive decisions. Use only the isolated
`demo-nexora-global-retail` tenant with `NEXORA_DEMO_MODE=true`.

## Opening — 3 minutes

**Say:** “Nexora is the operating system for enterprise technology decisions. It connects
technology, finance, risk, architecture, operations, and evidence so executives can decide
what to fund, what to simplify, and what risk to accept.”

Establish the demonstration boundary:

- All displayed records are deterministic synthetic demonstration data.
- Opportunity is never presented as realized value.
- Uncertified financial impact is shown as `UNKNOWN`.
- Every recommendation retains evidence coverage, confidence, and accountable authority.

## Journey 1 — Technology investment — 10 minutes

**Executive question:** Should we fund the modernization of Global Digital Checkout?

1. Open **CEO Workspace** and orient to $214M technology investment, 340 business services,
   and four leadership decisions.
2. Use **Business service health** to identify Digital Checkout as the weakest material service.
3. Open `NXR-INV-204` under **Three decisions, one enterprise story**.
4. Explain the dependency: two end-of-support technologies and concentrated payment-provider
   exposure create peak-season continuity risk.
5. Show the recommendation: an $8.2M phased modernization with migration controls.
6. Move to **CIO Workspace** to show technology health, governed technologies, portfolio
   disposition, and accountable architecture action.
7. Close in **CFO Workspace**: the investment is reviewed against the certified baseline and
   value pipeline—not against invented savings.

**Decision close:** CIO sponsors the option, CFO validates the baseline, and the Risk Committee
confirms migration controls.

## Journey 2 — Portfolio rationalization — 8 minutes

**Executive question:** Where can we simplify the estate without weakening business services?

1. In **CIO Workspace**, use **Technology portfolio disposition** to distinguish strategic,
   tolerate, modernize, and retire populations.
2. Open `NXR-PORT-118` and explain the overlap across 38 collaboration and analytics SaaS
   subscriptions.
3. In **CFO Workspace**, use **Vendor concentration** and **Value realization funnel** to show
   why commercial opportunity must move through evidence qualification, approval, execution,
   and verification.
4. Emphasize that $12.4M is qualified opportunity while $3.1M is verified realized value.

**Decision close:** Finance assigns benefit ownership, Technology confirms service migration,
and Procurement sequences renewals.

## Journey 3 — Executive technology risk — 8 minutes

**Executive question:** What technology exposure requires leadership action now?

1. In **CEO Workspace**, connect the Digital Checkout outcome to Payments Authorization risk.
2. Open `NXR-RISK-071` and show the explicit provider dependency and residual unknowns.
3. State clearly that financial impact is `UNKNOWN`; Nexora will not fabricate a loss estimate.
4. Use **Recommendation pipeline** to show where the mitigation waits for evidence, executive
   review, approval, execution, and outcome verification.

**Decision close:** Risk accepts or mitigates the exposure, Operations rehearses failover, and
Finance certifies impact only when evidence exists.

## Board-ready close — 4 minutes

Summarize the connected story:

> One revenue-critical service revealed a resilience investment, a portfolio simplification
> opportunity, and a concentration risk. Nexora connected each signal to financial context,
> evidence, authority, and the next accountable action.

Then ask the buyer:

1. Which technology decision currently takes your organization weeks to assemble?
2. Where do Finance, Architecture, Risk, and Operations disagree on evidence?
3. Which decision would be valuable enough to validate in a bounded pilot?

## Demonstration guardrails

- Do not represent synthetic figures as customer benchmarks or promised outcomes.
- Do not claim savings before verified realization.
- Do not bypass tenant or role boundaries for convenience.
- Do not improvise missing financial values; use `UNKNOWN`.
- Record product feedback separately from release-blocking defects.
